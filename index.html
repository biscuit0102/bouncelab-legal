Privacy Policy

Bounce Lab ("the game") is made by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and you don't need an account. To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, it records anonymous play events:

WhatExamples
A random play IDA random code made when the game is first opened. It isn't linked to you, your Apple ID or Google account, or your device's advertising ID, and it's deleted if you delete the game.

Who processes it

Play events are sent to PostHog (PostHog Inc.), an analytics service, and stored on its servers in the European Union. PostHog processes them only on our behalf. We use them to see which levels are too hard or too easy, where players stop, and whether features work. We don't sell this information or use it to identify you.

Purchases are handled entirely by the store you got the game from: Apple's App Store on iPhone and iPad, Google Play on Android. We never see your payment details; the store tells the game only that a purchase was made, so it can deliver what you bought.

Ads: the game may show ads. When an advertising partner is added, this policy will name it and explain what it collects, and the game will ask your permission before any tracking where your device requires it (on iPhone and iPad), and respect your device's ad settings (on Android).

Your choices

  • Switch it off: in the game, open Settings and turn off Share play data. The game then stops sending play events, and anything not yet sent is deleted.
  • Delete it: deleting the game removes the random play ID and everything stored on your device. Because the events we receive aren't tied to you, we can't single yours out afterwards.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the EU or UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your information. Contact us to use them. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Legal basis and keeping it

We process anonymous play events on the basis of our legitimate interest in improving the game, and you can object at any time with the switch above. Play events are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted.
